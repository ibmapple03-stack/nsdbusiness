# Staff operating guide (Stage 1)

This guide is for academy staff using the administration area at `/admin`. Times are Nigeria time (WAT) unless marked otherwise. The CEO's screens also show New York time where it matters.

## Signing in

1. Go to `/login` and enter your work email and password.
2. **First sign-in:** you will be asked to set up two-step sign-in. Install an authenticator app on your phone (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 2FAS), scan the QR code, and enter the 6-digit code.
3. **Save your backup codes** somewhere private. Each one works once if you lose your phone.
4. New staff receive an email with a link to set their password. The link expires in 24 hours. If it has expired, use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page.

## How publishing works

Nothing appears on the public website until it has been **submitted**, **approved by someone else**, and **published**.

| Status | Meaning | Who moves it on |
|---|---|---|
| Draft | Being written; can be edited | Editor → *Submit for approval* |
| Awaiting approval | Waiting for a decision | Approver (not the submitter) → *Approve* or *Return for changes* (reason required) |
| Returned for changes | Edit and resubmit | Editor |
| Approved, not published | Ready, not yet public | Approver → *Publish* |
| Published | Live on the website | Approver → *Unpublish* or *Archive* (reason required) |

To change something that is published, an approver chooses **Move back to draft**. This removes it from the website until it is approved and published again. For policies, use **Start a new version** instead: the current version stays live while the new one is prepared.

## Common tasks

### Prepare a course (Academy Manager)
1. **Courses → open the draft course** and complete every field. The **certificate type** must say exactly what is issued and by whom. Do not mention accreditation unless the academy holds written approval.
2. **Submit for approval.** The Coordinator or CEO approves and publishes it.

### Schedule a cohort (Academy Manager)
1. **Cohorts & timetable → New cohort.** Choose the course, dates, venue, instructor and places.
2. **Propose the fee** on the cohort page with a reason. A Coordinator or the CEO must approve it, and the proposer can never approve their own fee.
3. **Add timetable sessions.** The system refuses a session that clashes with another cohort's use of the same venue or instructor.
4. Submit the cohort. It can be published only after its course is published.
5. When applications open, change **Cohort stage** to *Open for applications*. This can be done while the cohort is published.

### Publish verified contact details (Coordinator/CEO)
**Contact details** → enter the value → **Save** → **Mark as verified and publish**. Only verified values appear on the website. Changing a value hides it again until it is re-verified.

### Handle an enquiry (Secretary)
**Enquiries** → open the message → reply by email from the academy mailbox → set the status and an internal note (for example, "Replied 3 Nov, sent course list").

### Approve a profile photo (Coordinator/CEO)
**People** → open the profile → **Approve photo for publication**, with a note recording how the person gave approval. Profiles and photos are approved separately.

### Add a staff member (CEO)
**Users & access → Add a staff member.** Give the fewest roles needed. They receive a set-password email. To remove access, open the person and **Deactivate**. This signs them out immediately.

## Do not

- Publish names, photos, titles or biographies that the person has not approved.
- Add qualifications, awards, partners, testimonials, statistics or accreditation that have not been confirmed in writing.
- Store information about SABFI beneficiaries, staff or finances in this system.
- Share your password, authenticator or backup codes. Every action is recorded under your name.

## Audit log

The CEO and Coordinator can see every sensitive action under **Audit log**, including who acted, when and why. Entries cannot be edited or deleted from the application.
